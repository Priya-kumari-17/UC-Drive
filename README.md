# UC-Drive
UC Drive's Prototype

An interactive prototype of UC Drive, a new Urban Company service that sends a verified driver to drive the customer's own car.

## Open it
Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No install needed.

## What's inside
- **Customer app:** UC Drive next to InstaHelp on the home screen. Book a driver In 1 hr, Later or Multi-day. Car make, model and transmission are required so the driver matches the car. Repeat users can book the same driver again. Also includes the trip flow (handover photos, PIN, live tracking, SOS), ratings, damage claims, wallet and account menus.
- **Partner app:** accept or decline In 1 hr requests, availability, the car types a driver is cleared for, and driver onboarding.
- **Ops console:** bookings by zone and type, manual assignment, safety desk, drivers, claims, per-zone service switches and the rate card.

All people, prices and bookings in the prototype are sample data.
